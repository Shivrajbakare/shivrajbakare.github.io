      01 About me

      Atoms by degree, models by choice.

      I’m studying Materials Science at IIT Kanpur. Somewhere between learning how atoms bond and running DFT, I got curious whether a neural network could learn those bonds straight from data, and I’ve been doing ML ever since.

      I’ve built graph networks for crystals, deepfake and AI-image detectors, and a Sanskrit speech recogniser. I like models that can show why they gave an answer, and systems that keep working once they’re shipped.
